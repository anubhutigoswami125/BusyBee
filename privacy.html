Privacy Policy.

LAST UPDATED: 29 OCTOBER 2026

BusyBee is a personal deadline calendar. This page explains what information the current version stores, where it is stored, and what you should know before using it.

In short: deadlines and subject settings you enter are saved in your browser on the device you are using. BusyBee provides backup and restore tools so you can manage a copy yourself.

1. Information you enter

You may enter deadline titles, dates, subjects, urgency levels, notes, and subject colour preferences. These details may reveal information about your education, schedule, or personal life, so avoid putting passwords, financial information, or other sensitive details in deadline notes.

2. Where the information is stored

The current calendar saves its deadline data in your browser's local storage. It is intended to remain on that browser and device and is not designed to synchronise your deadlines between devices. Anyone with access to your browser profile or device may be able to access the saved information.

BusyBee's hosting provider delivers the website. The hosting provider may process technical information such as connection data or request logs as part of providing and securing the service. This policy does not make a promise about the hosting provider's independent data practices; consult the provider's own policies for those details.

3. Backups and restoration

The Back Up feature creates a JSON file containing your BusyBee subject settings and deadlines. The file is downloaded to a location controlled by you. Restore reads a backup file you select and replaces the current calendar data after confirmation. Keep backup files somewhere secure, because they may contain personal schedule information.

4. Retention and deletion

Your saved calendar data generally remains in the browser until you delete entries, clear the site's browser storage, or remove the browser profile. Deleting the website's local storage may permanently remove your calendar data if you have not kept a backup. Downloaded backup files must be deleted separately.

5. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The current BusyBee calendar code does not intentionally use advertising trackers or analytics to profile calendar activity. The hosting platform or website delivery infrastructure may use essential technical mechanisms to provide, protect, or operate the site. This statement describes the current app code and should be reviewed if integrations or tracking tools are added later.

6. Children and educational information

BusyBee is a general-purpose planning tool, not a school records system. Do not enter another person's private information unless you have a proper reason and permission to do so.

7. Security and limitations

Local storage is not an encrypted vault and should not be treated as a secure document store. Use a device you trust, keep your browser updated, and protect any downloaded backup. No website or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when BusyBee's features or data practices change. The date at the top indicates when this page was last updated. Material changes should be reflected here before the updated feature is made publicly available.

9. Contact

For privacy questions about BusyBee, contact the project owner through the contact method published alongside the project. A direct contact address has not been added to this page yet.
